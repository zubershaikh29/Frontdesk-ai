A voice receptionist that takes clinic calls in Hindi and English, books appointments into a real calendar, and grades its own performance on every call.

Built on Retell AI. Below is a transcript from a development call.

00:00 agentSunrise Dental, good afternoon. How can I help you?
00:03 callerHaan, mujhe kal evening ka appointment chahiye, cleaning ke liye.
00:07 tool callcheck_availability(date: 2026-09-14, after: "17:00", duration: 30)
00:08 agentKal shaam ko 5:30 aur 6:15 free hai. Kaunsa theek rahega?
00:12 caller6:15 kar dijiye.
00:14 tool callbook_appointment(slot: 18:15, name: …, phone: …) → confirmed
00:16 agentDone — kal 6:15 pm. Confirmation WhatsApp par bhej diya hai.

The caller switches language mid-sentence. Handling that without dropping the booking is most of the work.

What it does

Answers in two languages
Hindi and English, including callers who mix both in one sentence
Books for real
Reads live availability and writes to Google Calendar, with slot locking so two callers can't take the same time
Knows when to stop
Hands off to a human on repeated failure, frustration, or direct request — with a written summary of the call so far
Grades every call
A judge model scores each transcript on six dimensions and flags the bad ones for review
Tests without calling
Caller personas replay against the agent in text, so prompt changes get checked in CI instead of over the phone
